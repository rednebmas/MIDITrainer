# Privacy Policy for MIDITrainer

**Last updated: December 27, 2024**

## Overview

MIDITrainer is an ear training app that helps musicians improve their ability to recognize and play back melodies. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.**

MIDITrainer:
- Does not require an account or login
- Does not collect analytics or usage data
- Does not use any third-party tracking services
- Does not transmit any data over the internet
- Does not access your contacts, photos, or other personal information

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only. This includes:
- Your practice settings (key, scale, tempo preferences)
- Your practice history and statistics
- MIDI device preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

## MIDI Device Access

The app requires access to MIDI devices (via USB or Bluetooth) solely for the purpose of:
- Receiving your keyboard input during practice
- Sending MIDI output for melody playback

No data from your MIDI devices is stored or transmitted.

## Bluetooth

If you use a Bluetooth MIDI device, the app will request Bluetooth permission. This is used exclusively for connecting to your MIDI keyboard and is not used for any tracking or data collection purposes.

## Third-Party Services

MIDITrainer does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## Children's Privacy

MIDITrainer does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
